Two routes to one linked record

Our six-regulator review proposed following three things together after a digital therapy is authorised. Both devices below are designed to record all three for the same person, so post-market surveillance can see them together.

1 · Software version2 · Patient engagement3 · Clinical outcomes
ROUTE 1 · PILOT FIRST

Smart insulin pen + worn CGM hub

  • Reusable motorised pen docks into a worn hub (CGM + soft cannula), so doses go in without new skin punctures
  • The person decides and confirms every dose; strip meter as a backup
  • Includes a virtual test bench, specs and a cost model (simulation, not evidence)
Open the pen in 3D →
ROUTE 2 · GEN 1 TARGET

CGM + insulin patch

  • Reusable transmitter on a disposable 3-day pod; one press inserts a CGM and a soft insulin cannula 10–12 mm apart
  • Glucose every 5 minutes and every insulin delivery, recorded automatically
  • Built-in safety rules for signal loss, site failure and under-filling

Our approach: staged, one data model

Prior-art checkMap existing smart pens, meters and patches; claim no novelty before this
Virtual benchSimulate both routes (the pen page already has one) to find weak points before hardware
Bench + sterilisationHub and pod: insertion, sensor–insulin spacing, dose accuracy, occlusion
Feasibility, then pilotAnimal model and human feasibility; pilot the pen first (6–12 months), then the patch
Post-marketBoth routes followed with our surveillance framework

Because both routes share the same data model, what the pen pilot teaches about version, use and outcomes carries straight into the patch.

Please read: these are our concepts, shown as illustrative 3D models. Numbers are design targets; nothing has been built, bench-tested or clinically tested. The pen’s dose advisor would stay off in our pilot until clinically validated; adverse-event reporting continues. Regulatory routes must be confirmed with each regulator. This site collects no data.
