What it does

One wearable patch, applied with one press, that measures glucose continuously and delivers insulin through two separate sites 10–12 mm apart.

Data it creates

Glucose every 5 minutes, every insulin delivery, pod changes and signal gaps, each stamped with the firmware and app version.

Link to our review

Our review found that software version, patient use and clinical outcomes are rarely linked for the same patients over time. This design creates that linked record from the first day of wear.

Design targets from the team's Gen 1 reference, not measured results. Thresholds are starting values for bench tuning. Simulated values only.

FROM REVIEW TO DESIGN

How the patch follows our framework

Our six-regulator review proposed following three things together after authorisation: software version, patient engagement and clinical outcomes. The patch is designed to record all three for the same person.

1 · Software versionFirmware and app version stamped on every glucose reading and every insulin delivery.
2 · Patient engagementWear time, pod changes, insulin delivered and signal gaps. A gap is flagged, not assumed to be non-use.
3 · Clinical outcomesContinuous glucose every 5 minutes; HbA1c still from the laboratory.
Safety, built inNeedles retract and lock; staged fallback on signal loss; two-site cross-check.

Safety rules (starting values)

RuleTriggerAction
Staged fallback, Stage AGlucose signal lostBasal at 50% for up to 2 h; ask for a fingerstick
Staged fallback, Stage BMore than 2 h without glucose dataSuspend basal; alarm: change pod or use backup pen
Insulin-site cross-checkGlucose high and rising for 60 min on the sensor site despite delivered insulin, no occlusion"Insulin site suspect" alert; automatic increases capped
Fill checkPlunger position when the pod is attachedReports units loaded and days covered; warns if under-filled

How we will test it

BenchSensor drift vs spacing; inserter fire, retract and lock reliability; fill and delivery accuracy.
SterilisationGlucose-enzyme sensitivity after low-dose e-beam.
Animal model3-day wear, sensor accuracy and interference at the chosen spacing.
Human feasibilityOpen-loop, 3-day wear, sensor accuracy and insulin-site survival.
